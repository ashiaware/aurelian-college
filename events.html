    Planning your visit

    Tickets and access

    PricesMostly free

    Most events are free. Mainstage productions are $15 for the public, $8 for seniors and children, and free for Aurelian students, staff, and Promise families.

    Accessible performances

    Every Mainstage show has a captioned and an audio-described performance, and a relaxed performance. Wheelchair spaces can be booked at any price.

    Getting here

    Visitor parking and the free shuttle stop are beside the Library. See the campus map.

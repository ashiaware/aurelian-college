Jobs & internships

The Stage Door Office finds paid campus jobs, sets up internships, and gets your work in front of agents, galleries, and studios.

Drop in at the stage door of the Aurelian Theatre, weekdays 10am to 6pm, or ask Tamsin Clarke's team a question.

What the Stage Door Office does

Campus jobs

Paid shifts from $16 an hour. Work-study students are matched first, and anyone can apply.

Internships

Placements with companies, studios, and museums. Up to 3 elective credits; required for Arts Management in third year.

Auditions and portfolios

Headshots, reels, audition coaching, and portfolio reviews before you send work out.

After you graduate

Alumni can use the office for life, including the job board and mock auditions.

Paid, on campus

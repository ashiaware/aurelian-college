House rules & guests

Friends and family are welcome. Every guest is signed in, so the Porter's Lodge always knows who is in the building.

Up to 3 day visitorsAt a time, 8am to 11pm
1 overnight guestPer student, per night
3 nights a weekNever more than 3 nights in a row
Photo IDEvery guest, every visit. No overnight guests in jury weeks.

How to bring a guest

  1. 1

    Check the limits

    You can sign in up to 3 day visitors at a time, and 1 overnight guest a night. In an Ensemble, the whole suite can host at most 2 overnight guests on the same night.

  2. 2

    Ask your roommates

    Everyone you share with has to agree to an overnight guest, and anyone can say no without giving a reason.

  3. 3

    Register your guest by 9pm

    In MyAurelian under Guests, or at the Porter's Lodge desk. You get a guest pass number right away.

  4. 4

    Your guest signs in at the Lodge

    They show a photo ID and the pass number, and get a guest wristband. Guests under 18 also need a form signed by a parent or guardian.

  5. 5

    Stay together in the House wing

    Guests can't go into studios or other wings without you, and you are responsible for them the whole visit.

  6. 6

    Sign out when they leave

    Your guest hands the wristband back at the Lodge. Day visitors leave by 11pm.

The rest of the House rules

Studios at any hourOpen 24/7

Dance studios, practice rooms, the drawing room, design labs, the darkroom, the edit suites, and the Library reading room are open 24 hours to every student with a House card. The Forge, the glass hot shop, and the aerial rigs need a trained student monitor after 10pm. The campus shuttle runs until 2am.

Quiet hours11pm to 7am

11pm to 7am on weeknights, and from midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. Dance, Circus, and early-technique floors start quiet hours at 10pm.

Practicing in your roomHeadphones

Headphones only for amplified instruments. Acoustic practice belongs in the 40 practice rooms in the West Cloister, open 24 hours. No soldering, torches, kilns, or spray paint in rooms.

Rooms over breaksOpen all year

Halls stay open over the winter and spring breaks at no cost. Summer housing is $140 a week for students in summer jobs, internships, or the Summer Concert.

Traditions

  • Welcome Night

    Your first evening. Each House meets its new members at the gate and walks them in by lantern light.

  • Matriculation

    That Saturday, first-years process into the chapel in gowns and sign the College register.

  • Friday Formal Dinner

    Every Friday of term: gowns, candlelight, and a toast at each House table.

  • The Porter's Lodge

    At the main gate, day and night: keys, mail, lost property, and directions.

  • The Library After Dark

    The reading room never closes. After midnight it is lit only by green-shaded lamps.

  • Commemoration Dinner

    The last Formal Dinner of the year, when the Muses' Cup is presented.

  • The Spring Runway

    Fashion students close Spring Open Studios with a runway down the Long Gallery.

  • Big Top Night

    Circus Arts takes over the Circus Barn every Summer term, with live music from the Cellar Club band.

Accessibility

Our buildings are old. Getting into them shouldn't be hard.

Everything about accessibility

Accessibility Services:access@aurelianarts.edu
Getting around
  • Step-free routes into every historic building, with ramps and lifts
  • Accessible parking with priority spots near each House wing
  • Free campus shuttle and paratransit
In the studios
  • Sprung dance floors and an adapted aerial program
  • Adjustable benches and seated soldering stations at the Forge, and an accessible wheel in the Kiln House
  • Height-adjustable drafting and cutting tables and screen-reader-friendly software
In class
  • Captioned lectures and audio-described slides
  • Extra time or alternative formats for juries and reviews
  • Accessible transport on museum trips
Living and dining
  • An accessible version of every floor plan in every wing, at the same price
  • Quiet sensory-friendly floors and animal-approved rooms
  • Reachable counters, tray service, and custom plans for medical diets

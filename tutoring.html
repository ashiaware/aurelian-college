For multilingual students

English support

Your art matters more than your accent. If English is your second or third language, all of this is free, for as long as you want it.

More for international students

  • English for the Arts, freeA four-week summer course in New York before your first term, with housing and meals included. You learn the words of your art (rehearsal notes, critiques, contracts) and settle in early.
  • Weekly tutoringOne-on-one sessions with an English tutor all year, as many as you want, at no cost. Bring an essay, a script, or a scene.
  • The Writing & Speaking CenterHelp with papers, artist statements, and practice for juries and interviews, open evenings in the Library.
  • Conversation partnersWe pair you with a student from your House for coffee and practice twice a week. Most pairs become friends.
  • Extra time and support in classExtra time on written exams and your choice of a dictionary in class. Your Fellow can give critiques in writing as well as out loud.
  • Help in your languageOrientation guides in eight languages, and counselors who speak Spanish, Mandarin, Korean, French, and Portuguese.

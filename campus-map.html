Visit

Campus map

On Morningside Heights in Manhattan, above Riverside Park, Ashcombe Hall wraps around the Great Quad, with each House wing beside its own studios. Select any building to see what is inside and when it is open.

Visiting? Start at the Porter's Lodge at the main gate on Chapel Street. Accessible parking is beside every House wing, and visitor parking is beside the Library.

Main Gate and Porter's Lodge

Where every visit starts. Keys, mail, lost property, security, and campus tours at 11am and 2pm.

Hours: Open 24 hours

Parking & getting here

Parking, shuttles, and drop-off

Select a P on the map above to see each lot. Most visitors come by the 1 train; if you drive, park in the Visitor Lot on Chapel Street.

$3 an hour

Visitor parking

The Visitor Parking Lot on Chapel Street, beside the Library. Free on weekends, on Visit Day, and for auditions. Electric-car chargers in the first row.

Free

Accessible parking

15 accessible spaces beside the North Cloister, the South Tower, and the East Tower, each next to a step-free door. Use a disability placard or ask Accessibility Services for a College permit.

$420 a term

Students with cars

A limited number of student permits for the Visitor Lot overnight. Most students don't need a car: your subway and bus card is free.

Permit

Faculty and staff

The Staff Lot behind the Scene Shop, entered from West 122nd Street. Permits from Facilities.

Free

Shuttle and subway

The College shuttle runs to the 1 train at 116th and 125th Streets every 10 minutes until 2am. The stop is beside Admissions.

Always open

Drop-off and rideshare

Use the Drop-off bay on Chapel Street, a short walk from the Porter's Lodge. Paratransit stops here too.

Free

Bikes

Covered, locked storage for 120 bikes beside the East Tower. Citi Bike docks on Riverside Drive and Broadway.

Free after 6pm

Show nights

The Visitor Lot is free after 6pm on performance nights. When it fills, the Riverside garage on West 125th Street gives Aurelian ticket holders $10 parking.

Where we are

Morningside Heights, Manhattan

Our campus sits on the bluffs above Riverside Park and the Hudson River, on Manhattan's Upper West Side. Broadway is 25 minutes away on the 1 train, and students intern at theaters, galleries, studios, and fashion houses all over the city.

To Broadway

Take the 1 train from 116 St to Times Sq–42 St: about 25 minutes. Student rush tickets for most shows are $35 to $45, and the College covers two a term.

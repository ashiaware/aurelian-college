Find your way

One square block in Morningside Heights

Every studio, stage, and House wing sits around the Great Quad, a five-minute walk end to end. The 1 train stops two blocks away at 116th Street.

  • Porter's LodgeStart every visit here, at the main gate on Chapel Street. Open 24 hours.
  • The Great QuadThe lawn at the heart of campus, ringed by the four House wings.
  • The Aurelian TheatreOur 600-seat main stage, beside the Black Box and the Scene Shop.
  • Visitor parkingOn Chapel Street beside the Library. Free on weekends and on Visit Day.

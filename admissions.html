What to prepare

Auditions & portfolios

Choose your family of art to see what your program asks for.

Audition on campus on any Saturday from January 30 to February 27, or send a video by February 27.

  • Dance: ballet barre, a contemporary combination, and a short solo
  • Musical Theater: a 32-bar song, a one-minute monologue, and a dance call
  • Physical Theatre & Circus Arts: a movement class, a two-minute skill or physical piece, and an interview. No circus experience needed.

Audition in person or by video. Pianists are provided.

  • Instrumental Performance: two contrasting pieces and sight-reading
  • Voice & Opera: two contrasting art songs or arias, one not in English
  • Jazz & Contemporary Music: a standard with an improvised chorus, or two original songs
  • Composition & Production: three recorded or notated pieces
  • MFA Musical Theater Writing: a scene with two songs

Performers audition; everyone else sends work and talks with faculty for 15 minutes.

  • Acting: two contrasting monologues, under two minutes each
  • Directing: a director's concept for a play of your choice, and an interview
  • Creative Writing & Playwriting: 10 to 20 pages of plays, prose, or poems
  • Film & Cinematography: a short film or reel under five minutes
  • Theatre Design & Technology: designs, models, or photos of shows you worked on
  • Arts Management: an essay and the interview

Upload a portfolio of your strongest recent work. Unfinished work and sketchbooks are welcome.

  • Fine Arts, Illustration, Jewelry, Ceramics & Glass: 12 to 20 pieces, including drawing from life
  • Photography: 15 to 20 photographs
  • Fashion & Textile Design: 10 to 20 pieces, sketches or garments
  • Interaction & Graphic Design, Architectural Design: 10 to 15 pieces of any kind of making
  • Art History: an essay of 1,000 to 2,000 words

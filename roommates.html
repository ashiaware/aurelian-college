How the Roommate Board works

Run by the Senior Tutor's office and the Floor Prefects of each House.

Every match is mutual

You can invite anyone in your House, and they choose whether to accept. A group locks only when everyone has said yes, and nobody sees who turned down an invitation.

Who you can room with

Members of your own House, in the same plan: Ensembles of five for first-years, Duets of two from second year. Solo rooms have no roommate, and you can request one from second year in the room ballot.

DeadlinesJuly 1

First-years lock their Ensemble by July 1. Returning students lock Duets by the room ballot in week 6 of Summer term. Anyone without a full group is placed by their Head of House using the same questions, so no one is left out.

Living agreementsWeek 2

In week 2 every room fills in a one-page agreement with its Floor Prefect: sleep, cleaning, practice, and guests. Overnight guests can stay up to three nights a week, only if every roommate agrees.

If it isn't working

Talk to your Floor Prefect first. If things don't improve, the House Tutor can move you to another room in your wing at any point in the year, at no cost.

Safety and privacyReport button

Only first names, majors, and the answers you choose appear. Report any profile and the Head of House reviews it within a day. Access needs and roommate requests for medical reasons go privately to Accessibility Services instead.

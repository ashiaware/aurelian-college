What we studied

Research

Real college websites and public sources that shaped what this site includes. Nothing on them is copied.

  • The Juilliard SchoolAudition-first admissions, performance calendar, box office
  • Rhode Island School of Design (RISD)Portfolio requirements, majors by department, campus map
  • Berklee College of MusicOnline auditions, scholarship language, student life
  • Royal College of ArtNews and alumni pages, graduate showcase
  • California Institute of the Arts (CalArts)Interdisciplinary programs, "Information for" audience menus
  • School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC)Pre-college programs, resources hub, accreditation and policy pages
  • Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)How the FAFSA and federal aid work
  • U.S. Department of Education, College Affordability and Transparency CenterWhy colleges publish a net price calculator
  • MTA subway mapSubway routes and travel times from Morningside Heights
  • Lincoln Center for the Performing ArtsThe performing-arts campus students visit for classes and shows
